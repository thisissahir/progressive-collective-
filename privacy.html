Privacy Policy

Last updated · June 2026

Progressive Collective (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit progressivecollective.in or contact us, and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you contact us by form, email or phone, we collect the details you choose to share — such as your name, email address, phone number and project details. We may also collect basic, anonymous analytics about how visitors use the site (pages viewed, device type, approximate location) to improve it.

How we use it

We use your information solely to respond to enquiries, deliver our services, send project-related communication, and improve our website. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Third-party content

Our site embeds content from services such as Spotify, YouTube, Vimeo and SoundCloud. These providers may set their own cookies and collect usage data under their own privacy policies. Media and images are served via content delivery networks.

Data retention

We keep enquiry information only as long as needed to respond to and service your request, or as required by law.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you at any time by emailing us.
